About Allofwind Digital

A software subscription, information, and affiliate business operated by Allofwind LLC.

What we do

Allofwind Digital publishes software buying information and offers selected fixed-term digital software subscriptions. The current direct catalogue includes AutoCAD, 3ds Max, and Inventor products.

How we earn revenue

Revenue may come from direct digital product sales and disclosed affiliate commissions from eligible third-party transactions.

Operating principles

  • Products, USD prices, terms, and delivery conditions are published.
  • Direct sales and affiliate referrals are separated.
  • Customer support channels and response targets are visible.
  • Third-party trademarks are not presented as our own.
