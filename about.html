Operating principles

Accuracy before appearance.

1Do not imply official status. Brand ownership, authorisation, partnership, and endorsement claims require evidence.
2Disclose commercial relationships. Affiliate links should have a clear, nearby disclosure.
3Identify the seller. Customers should know who receives payment and handles support.
4State delivery and refund terms. Direct products need product-specific information before checkout.
