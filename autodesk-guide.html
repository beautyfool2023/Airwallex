Decision framework

Six areas to confirm.

Workflow and product

Identify the design, drafting, engineering, construction, manufacturing, or media workflow you need to support.

Named users

Confirm how many individual users need access and whether user assignment or account administration is required.

System requirements

Check operating system, processor, memory, graphics, storage, network, and peripheral requirements.

Files and collaboration

Confirm required file formats, interoperability, cloud collaboration, versioning, and team workflows.

Licence conditions

Review subscription duration, commercial use, education or trial restrictions, region, renewal, and cancellation.

Seller and fulfilment

Identify who takes payment, delivers access, provides invoices, handles refunds, and supports the order.

Transaction responsibility

Confirm the merchant and licence conditions.

Purchases completed on a third-party destination are governed by that merchant’s transaction terms. Direct purchases identify Allofwind LLC as the seller and use our direct-sale policies.

Do not assume that activation alone proves a licence is suitable for your intended commercial use.
