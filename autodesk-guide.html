Independent guidance for choosing an Autodesk product, release, fixed term, compatible system, and eligible account.

Choose the exact product

AutoCAD, 3ds Max, and Inventor support different professional workflows. Review the product description and system requirements rather than selecting only by price.

Confirm the release

The product pages permit a request for 2025, 2026, or 2027. Confirm the exact entitlement and compatibility before payment.

Confirm the fixed term

The listed direct-sale products offer 1, 2, and 3-year terms with one-time USD prices and no automatic renewal.

Account and country

An eligible Autodesk account and supported country may be required. The intended customer markets are the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, and Australia.
