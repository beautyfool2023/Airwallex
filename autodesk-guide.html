Questions to review before purchasing

  • Which exact Autodesk product and edition is required?
  • Is the subscription for a named individual, team, business, or eligible education user?
  • What is the subscription duration and renewal arrangement?
  • Does the device meet published system requirements?
  • Are there regional or account restrictions?
  • Who handles activation, billing, cancellation, and refunds?
