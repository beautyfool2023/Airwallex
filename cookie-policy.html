Cookie Policy

How cookies and similar technologies may be used for site operation, analytics, and affiliate attribution.

Effective date: 6 August 2026

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files placed on a device by a website or service provider. Similar technologies may include local storage, pixels, and identifiers.

How we may use them

  • Essential: security, routing, and basic website functionality.
  • Analytics: understanding page usage and performance.
  • Affiliate attribution: allowing a merchant or network to identify that a visitor arrived through an affiliate link.
  • Preference: remembering settings where implemented.

Third-party cookies

Third-party merchants, affiliate networks, analytics providers, and payment providers may use their own technologies under their own policies.

Your choices

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser. Blocking cookies may affect attribution, preferences, or some website functionality.
