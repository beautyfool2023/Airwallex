Cookie Policy

This policy describes cookies, affiliate tracking, and similar technologies that may be associated with Allofwind Digital or third-party destinations.

Last updated: August 6, 2026

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files or browser records used to remember settings, support security, measure activity, or attribute referrals. Similar technologies can include local storage, pixels, and URL parameters.

2. Current website functionality

The current static site does not require non-essential cookies for basic page navigation. The mobile navigation and copyright year run locally in the browser and do not require a user account.

3. Affiliate tracking

Affiliate links may include referral identifiers. The destination network or merchant may set cookies or use similar technologies to record that a visitor arrived through a qualifying link. The destination party’s cookie and privacy policies apply.

4. Payments and external services

Direct checkout, payment, fraud-prevention, customer support, or embedded services may use technologies controlled by their providers. Their notices should explain how those technologies operate.

5. Analytics and advertising

If analytics, advertising pixels, or consent-management tools are added, this policy and any consent interface should be updated before those tools are activated.

6. Browser controls

You can use browser settings to block or delete cookies. Blocking cookies may affect affiliate attribution or the operation of third-party checkout and service pages.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy may be sent to contact@webdailylifetyles.com.
