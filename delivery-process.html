Delivery Process

How a direct digital software order moves from product selection to electronic delivery and support.

1. SelectChoose product, release, and term.
2. ConfirmSupport confirms eligibility and final terms.
3. PayComplete payment through the approved route.
4. ReviewPayment and order details are checked.
5. DeliverInstructions are emailed to the order address.
6. SupportContact us about delivery or activation issues.

Delivery timing

The normal target is within one business day after successful payment and any required order verification. If a different time applies, it must be disclosed before payment.

Customers are responsible for entering a valid email address and checking spam or junk folders.

Order verification

Some orders may require confirmation of customer identity, billing information, country, Autodesk account eligibility, or product availability before delivery.

If an order cannot be fulfilled, the payment will be handled according to the published refund policy and applicable law.

Delivery records

Allofwind LLC may retain the order date, payment status, delivery timestamp, product and term supplied, customer communications, and relevant activation or redemption records for support, fraud prevention, accounting, and dispute handling.

Delayed or failed delivery

Contact contact@webdailylifetyles.com with the order email and transaction reference. Do not submit a duplicate order or chargeback before giving support a reasonable opportunity to locate and resolve the issue.

Affiliate purchases

Orders completed with an external merchant are delivered and supported by that merchant, not by Allofwind LLC.
