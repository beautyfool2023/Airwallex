Offer requirements

What a direct-sale page must disclose.

These standards help customers understand exactly what they are buying before payment. They also keep direct sales distinct from third-party affiliate referrals.

Product identity

Name, edition or access type, included files or services, licence or subscription duration, and relevant restrictions.

Commercial terms

Seller, price, currency, billing frequency, renewal terms, taxes where applicable, and cancellation method.

Fulfilment and support

Electronic delivery method, estimated timing, system requirements, support contact, and product-specific refund conditions.

Product integrity

Products must be represented accurately.

We do not present cracked software, shared accounts, misused educational or non-profit access, or unidentified licence sources as legitimate commercial products.

Third-party brand names are used only to identify or discuss products. Authorised-reseller, official-partner, or endorsement claims should appear only when current written evidence supports them.

No generic checkout pages

A payment button should not appear without a meaningful product description and the terms needed to evaluate the purchase.
