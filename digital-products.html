Direct-sale standards

Product pages are not substitutes for a complete checkout.

Before accepting payment, the checkout must show the final price, currency, subscription term, exact supported release, seller, delivery timing, and refund conditions.

Affiliate offer or direct sale?

A direct sale identifies Allofwind LLC as seller. An affiliate link sends the customer to a third-party merchant that controls payment, delivery, and refunds.
