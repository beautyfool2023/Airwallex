Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about our business model, transactions, product delivery, refunds, and customer support.

Are you Microsoft or Autodesk?

No. Allofwind Digital is an independent website operated by Allofwind LLC. Product and company names are used only to identify products being discussed.

How do affiliate links work?

We may earn a commission when a visitor follows a qualifying link and completes an eligible purchase with a third-party merchant. The merchant controls payment, fulfilment, refunds, and support.

How can I identify a direct sale?

A direct-sale page clearly names Allofwind LLC as seller and states the price, currency, delivery method, licence or subscription term, technical requirements, and refund conditions.

How are digital products delivered?

Depending on the product, delivery may occur by email, download link, licence key, activation instructions, account invitation, or customer account access.

Can I request a refund?

Refund eligibility depends on whether the product was delivered, downloaded, redeemed, or activated, as well as the reason for the request and applicable law. See the Refund & Delivery Policy.

What should I do if delivery fails?

Email support with the order email, transaction reference, product name, and any error message. We will review delivery and activation records.

Do you store card details?

Payment card details are typically handled by the payment processor rather than stored directly by this static website. The Privacy Policy explains the data we may receive and process.

Where is the company registered?

Allofwind LLC is registered in Wyoming, United States. The business address and contact details are available on the Contact page.
