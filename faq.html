Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about products, pricing, releases, delivery, refunds, seller responsibility, and support.

What products do you sell?

The current direct-sale catalogue lists Autodesk AutoCAD, Autodesk 3ds Max, and Autodesk Inventor digital subscription products.

What prices do you charge?

Each product page displays the 1, 2, and 3-year one-time prices in USD. Prices differ by product and term.

Which releases can I request?

The product pages allow customers to request 2025, 2026, or 2027. Final entitlement and availability are confirmed before payment.

Do subscriptions renew automatically?

No. The published direct-sale plans are fixed-term products with one-time payments and no automatic renewal.

How are products delivered?

Direct orders are delivered electronically by email after payment confirmation and any required order review.

How long does delivery take?

The normal target is within one business day after confirmed payment and verification, unless a different time is disclosed before payment.

Which countries do you serve?

The intended customer markets are the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, and Australia, subject to account and licence eligibility.

Can I request a refund?

Refund eligibility depends on delivery, redemption or activation status, product conformity, the reason for the request, and applicable law.

Who is the seller?

Allofwind LLC is the seller for direct products whose pages and order confirmations identify it as seller.

How do affiliate links work?

An external merchant processes an affiliate purchase and controls delivery, support, cancellation, and refunds. Allofwind LLC may receive a commission from an eligible transaction.

Are you Autodesk?

No. Allofwind Digital is operated by Allofwind LLC and does not present itself as an Autodesk-owned website.

How do I contact support?

Email contact@webdailylifetyles.com or call +1 307-279-4870. The target response time is within two business days.
