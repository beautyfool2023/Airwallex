Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about the company, affiliate referrals, direct digital sales, delivery, refunds, and support.

Who operates Allofwind Digital?

Allofwind Digital is the public-facing brand operated by Allofwind LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company.

Is Allofwind Digital Microsoft, Autodesk, or an official publisher website?

No. We are an independent business. Third-party names are used to identify or discuss products. We do not claim official, authorised-reseller, partner, or endorsed status unless current written evidence supports that statement.

How does the site make money?

Revenue may come from qualifying affiliate referrals and from selected digital products sold directly by Allofwind LLC. Affiliate relationships are disclosed near relevant commercial links.

How can I tell who is selling a product?

For an affiliate referral, checkout occurs on the destination merchant’s website and that merchant is the seller. For a direct purchase, the offer and checkout identify Allofwind LLC as the seller.

How are direct digital products delivered?

Delivery may be by email, download link, licence or activation information, account invitation, or customer access page. The specific method and expected timing must appear on the product page.

What is the refund policy for a direct purchase?

Eligibility depends on the product, delivery or activation status, the stated product terms, and applicable law. Review the Refund, Cancellation & Digital Delivery Policy and the specific offer before checkout.

What happens if I bought through an affiliate link?

The destination merchant processes payment, fulfils the order, and controls refunds, renewals, warranty, and support. Contact the merchant shown on your receipt.

Do you store full payment-card numbers?

Direct payments may be processed by a third-party payment provider. Allofwind Digital does not request that customers send full card numbers or security codes by email.

How quickly does support respond?

The published target is within two business days during support hours. Investigations involving payment, delivery, fraud, or licence records may require additional time.

Can product information change?

Yes. Publishers and merchants may change plans, features, prices, eligibility, and terms. Verify current details on the offer or checkout page before buying.

Which Autodesk products are currently described on the site?

The current catalogue includes product information pages for AutoCAD, 3ds Max, and Inventor subscriptions. Availability, price, supported release, and subscription term must be confirmed in the final offer before payment.

Does a product page guarantee availability?

No. Availability can depend on the customer country, Autodesk account, selected release, licence eligibility, order review, and current supply. A completed checkout or written order confirmation is required.

When is Allofwind LLC the seller?

Allofwind LLC is the seller only when the offer and checkout identify it as seller and it processes the direct order. Links to external merchants are affiliate referrals and are governed by the merchant’s policies.

Need order help?

Use the support center to identify whether Allofwind Digital or a third-party merchant is responsible for your order.
