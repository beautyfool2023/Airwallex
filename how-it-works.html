Transaction clarity

Know whether you are buying from a third party or from Allofwind LLC.

Allofwind Digital supports two distinct commercial paths. The page and checkout should make the path visible before you pay.

Affiliate disclosureDirect-sale standards
1

Research the product

Use our guides to identify user, workflow, compatibility, licence, and renewal questions.

2

Identify the transaction type

Look for an affiliate disclosure or a statement that Allofwind LLC is the direct seller.

3

Review the offer terms

Confirm the product, price, currency, delivery, billing frequency, cancellation, refund, and support terms.

4

Use the correct support channel

Contact the merchant for affiliate purchases or Allofwind Digital for direct purchases.

Affiliate referral

Checkout occurs on a third-party website.

  • The outbound link may contain affiliate tracking.
  • The destination merchant sets the price and terms.
  • The merchant processes payment and delivers the product.
  • The merchant handles refunds, renewals, tax, and order support.
  • Allofwind LLC may receive a commission for qualifying activity.

Direct purchase

Allofwind LLC is identified as the seller.

  • The offer page states the product and included access.
  • Price, currency, billing, and delivery terms appear before payment.
  • A third-party payment processor may securely process the payment.
  • Allofwind Digital handles delivery and direct-order support.
  • The direct-sale refund and cancellation policy applies.

After payment

Use your receipt to confirm who sold the product.

The merchant name, payment descriptor, order confirmation, and delivery email should identify the responsible seller. Keep these records when contacting support.

Still uncertain?

Send us the page URL and a non-sensitive copy of the order details. Never email a full card number, password, or activation credential.
