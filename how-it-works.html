How It Works

A clear explanation of our content, affiliate referrals, direct sales, and customer-support responsibilities.

We research software categories

We publish educational content covering features, plan types, licensing questions, and buyer considerations.

We disclose commercial relationships

Selected outbound links may be affiliate links. We may receive a commission after an eligible transaction.

The transaction route is identified

Affiliate purchases occur on a third-party merchant site. Direct purchases identify Allofwind LLC as seller.

Support follows the seller

The destination merchant supports affiliate purchases. We support products sold directly by us.

Revenue model

We generate revenue through affiliate partnerships and selected direct digital product sales.

  • Affiliate compensation does not automatically affect our published price comparisons.
  • We do not guarantee merchant acceptance, availability, or pricing.
  • Direct-sale terms are disclosed before checkout.
