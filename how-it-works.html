How It Works

How direct product sales differ from affiliate recommendations, and how customers identify the seller before payment.

We publish product and guide information

Pages explain products, release considerations, prices, system requirements, and common buyer checks.

The seller is identified

A direct sale identifies Allofwind LLC as seller. An affiliate link identifies or leads to an external merchant.

Direct-order terms are confirmed

The customer confirms the product, release, fixed term, USD price, delivery, and refund conditions.

Electronic fulfilment follows payment

Direct orders are reviewed and delivered electronically under the published policies.

Revenue model

Revenue may come from direct digital product sales and from disclosed affiliate commissions on eligible third-party transactions.

Check the receipt: The seller shown in the offer, payment confirmation, and billing record determines who controls fulfilment and support.
