Questions to review

  • How many users need individual accounts?
  • Do users need desktop applications, web applications, or both?
  • What storage, collaboration, security, and administration features are required?
  • Is the plan intended for personal, family, business, education, or enterprise use?
  • What are the current renewal, cancellation, and regional eligibility terms?
