Microsoft 365 Buying Guide

Independent purchasing context for selecting a plan and understanding account, billing, and fulfilment requirements.

Brand notice: Allofwind Digital and Allofwind LLC are not Microsoft. Microsoft 365 and related names are trademarks of Microsoft. References do not imply sponsorship or endorsement.

Questions to review before purchasing

  • Is the plan intended for an individual, family, business, education, or enterprise user?
  • How many users and devices are supported?
  • Is billing monthly, annual, or prepaid for a fixed term?
  • Does the offer require a new or existing Microsoft account?
  • Are there geographic, tenant, language, or eligibility restrictions?
  • Who provides activation, billing support, and refunds?

Affiliate and direct-sale routes

Some links may lead to third-party merchants and may generate an affiliate commission. A direct-sale page, where available, will clearly identify Allofwind LLC as seller and display its own checkout, delivery, and refund terms.

Avoid unclear offers

Buyers should avoid listings that do not explain the licence term, user eligibility, account ownership, activation process, or support route. We do not promote shared accounts, educational licences for ineligible users, or unauthorised keys.
