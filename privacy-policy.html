Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Allofwind LLC may collect, use, share, and retain information through Allofwind Digital.

Last updated: August 6, 2026

1. Operator

Allofwind Digital is operated by Allofwind LLC. Contact us at contact@webdailylifetyles.com or through the details on the Contact page.

2. Information we may collect

Depending on the features used, we may collect information you submit directly; contact and support records; order and transaction references; basic device, browser, IP address, and page-interaction data; referral information; and records needed for security, fraud prevention, fulfilment, accounting, and legal compliance.

3. Affiliate links and third parties

Outbound links may contain tracking parameters used by affiliate networks or merchants to attribute referrals. A destination party may use its own cookies or similar technologies. Its privacy policy applies after you leave our website.

4. Direct payments

Direct payments may be processed by a third-party payment provider. We do not ask customers to send full payment-card numbers or security codes by email, and we do not intend to store full card details on this static website.

5. How information may be used

  • Operate, maintain, secure, and improve the website.
  • Respond to enquiries and provide direct-order support.
  • Process and document direct sales, delivery, refunds, and cancellations.
  • Measure referral and website performance.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, unauthorised access, and payment disputes.
  • Maintain accounting, tax, compliance, and legal records.

6. Sharing

Information may be shared when reasonably necessary with hosting, communications, payment, fraud-prevention, fulfilment, analytics, affiliate-network, professional-service, banking, and legal-compliance providers. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect rights, safety, or security.

7. Cookies and similar technologies

The current static website does not require non-essential cookies for basic navigation. Affiliate destinations, payment pages, or future analytics and embedded services may use cookies or similar technologies. See the Cookie Policy for details.

8. Retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described, including support, fulfilment, accounting, dispute, fraud-prevention, and legal obligations. We use proportionate administrative and technical safeguards, but no transmission or storage method is completely secure.

9. International processing

Service providers and business operations may process information in countries other than your own. Applicable safeguards and legal requirements may vary by location.

10. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to ask about personal information associated with you or to exercise rights available under applicable law. We may need to verify identity and may retain information where required for legal, security, transaction, or dispute purposes.

11. Children

The website is intended for business, professional, and general adult audiences and is not directed to children under 13.

12. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the website, services, or legal requirements change. Questions or requests may be sent to contact@webdailylifetyles.com.
