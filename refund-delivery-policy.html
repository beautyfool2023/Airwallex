Refund, Cancellation & Digital Delivery Policy

This policy explains delivery, cancellation, and refund terms for third-party affiliate purchases and products sold directly by Allofwind LLC.

Last updated: August 6, 2026

1. Affiliate purchases

When you follow an affiliate link and complete a purchase on a third-party merchant’s website, that merchant—not Allofwind LLC—processes payment, fulfils the order, and provides the product or service. The merchant’s delivery, renewal, cancellation, refund, return, warranty, privacy, and support policies apply.

2. Products sold directly by us

Allofwind LLC may sell selected software-related digital products, software licences, subscriptions, digital downloads, access credentials, or related digital services directly through Allofwind Digital. The product page should state the description, price and currency, licence or subscription period, system requirements, delivery method, estimated delivery time, and known restrictions.

Third-party product names and trademarks belong to their owners. Unless expressly stated and supported by current written evidence, reference does not mean that Allofwind LLC is the brand owner, authorised reseller, official partner, or endorsed seller.

3. Digital delivery

Direct-sale products are delivered electronically. Depending on the product, delivery may include a download link, licence or activation information, access credentials, an account invitation, redemption instructions, or access through a customer page.

Unless a product page states otherwise, delivery begins after payment authorisation and any required fraud or compliance checks. Some orders may require manual review. Delays may occur where payment verification, identity confirmation, regional eligibility, product availability, or additional customer information is required.

Customers are responsible for providing a valid email address and checking spam or junk folders. Contact us if the order has not arrived within the period stated on the product page.

4. Order accuracy and compatibility

Before purchase, review the product edition, licence or access type, term, supported operating system, language, geographic restrictions, account requirements, and other technical information shown on the product page. Contact us before purchasing if you are uncertain whether a product is suitable for your device, account, organisation, or intended use.

5. Refund eligibility

Direct-sale refund requests are assessed individually. Subject to applicable law, a refund may be approved where:

  • The customer was charged more than once for the same order.
  • We are unable to deliver the purchased product within the stated delivery period.
  • The delivered product is materially different from the description shown before checkout.
  • A licence or access credential supplied by us is invalid and we cannot provide a working replacement.
  • A technical issue caused by us prevents access and cannot be resolved within a reasonable period.
  • A refund is otherwise required under applicable consumer-protection law.

6. Delivered, accessed, or activated products

Because digital products can be copied, redeemed, downloaded, activated, or consumed immediately, change-of-mind refunds may not be available after a licence key has been revealed or redeemed; a subscription or account has been activated; a file has been downloaded or accessed; a personalised product has been created; or a service has been substantially performed.

This limitation does not exclude any refund, replacement, or remedy that cannot lawfully be excluded.

7. Refund requests

Email contact@webdailylifetyles.com with the order email, transaction reference, product name, explanation of the issue, and relevant non-sensitive screenshots or error messages. Do not include full card details, passwords, or reusable activation credentials.

Refund requests should normally be submitted within 14 calendar days of purchase or delivery. Later requests may still be reviewed where a product is defective, was not delivered as described, or applicable law provides a longer period.

We normally acknowledge requests within two business days. Investigation of activation, delivery, payment, fraud, or account records may require additional time.

8. Approved refunds

Approved refunds are normally returned to the original payment method. We generally cannot redirect a refund to a different person, card, bank account, or payment method. The payment provider or financial institution may require additional processing time.

Where a refund is granted, the associated licence, subscription, download permission, account access, or service entitlement may be cancelled or deactivated.

9. Subscription cancellations

For recurring subscriptions sold directly by us, the offer and checkout should disclose billing frequency, price, renewal terms, and cancellation method. Unless otherwise stated, cancellation prevents future renewal charges but does not automatically refund charges already processed. Access may continue until the end of the paid billing period.

Subscriptions purchased from a third-party merchant must be cancelled through that merchant.

10. Unauthorised or disputed payments

If you believe a direct payment was unauthorised, contact us promptly. We may request information reasonably necessary to identify and investigate the transaction. Contacting support first for delivery, activation, compatibility, cancellation, or refund issues gives us an opportunity to resolve the matter.

Nothing in this policy limits a customer’s right to contact a bank or payment provider about a genuinely unauthorised transaction.

11. Abuse and fraud

We may reject a refund request where there is reasonable evidence of fraud, refund abuse, unauthorised resale, account sharing, licence misuse, false information, or an attempt to retain access after a refund. Delivery may be suspended during payment, fraud, sanctions, identity, or compliance review.
