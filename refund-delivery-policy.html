Refund, Cancellation & Digital Delivery Policy

Terms for external affiliate purchases and digital software subscriptions sold directly by Allofwind LLC.

Effective date: 7 August 2026

1. Affiliate purchases

When a purchase is completed with an external merchant after following an affiliate link, that merchant controls payment, delivery, renewal, cancellation, refund, warranty, and support.

2. Direct digital products

Allofwind LLC sells selected fixed-term digital software subscriptions. Product pages state the USD price, term, release options, delivery method, system requirements, and material eligibility conditions.

3. Delivery

Direct orders are delivered electronically. The normal target is within one business day after successful payment and any required order verification.

4. Cancellation

The listed direct products are one-time payments for fixed terms and do not renew automatically. An order may be cancelled before delivery or fulfilment begins, subject to payment and verification status.

5. Refund eligibility

Subject to applicable law, a refund may be approved for duplicate charges, non-delivery, a materially misdescribed product, an invalid entitlement that cannot be replaced, or a technical issue caused by us that cannot be resolved within a reasonable time.

6. Delivered, redeemed, or activated products

Because digital products may be delivered, revealed, redeemed, activated, or otherwise consumed immediately, change-of-mind refunds may not be available after delivery or activation. This does not exclude rights that cannot legally be excluded.

7. Request process

Email contact@webdailylifetyles.com with the order email, transaction reference, product, release, term, explanation, and relevant screenshots. Requests should normally be submitted within 14 calendar days of purchase or delivery.

8. Response and processing

We normally acknowledge requests within two business days. Approved refunds are generally returned to the original payment method, and the financial institution may require additional processing time.

9. Unauthorised payments

Contact us promptly if a direct payment appears unauthorised. Nothing limits a customer's right to contact their bank or payment provider.
