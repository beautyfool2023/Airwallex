Refund, Cancellation & Digital Delivery Policy

This policy explains the delivery, cancellation, and refund terms applicable to third-party affiliate purchases and digital products sold directly by Allofwind LLC through Web Daily Lifetyles.

Some pages on this website contain affiliate links. When you follow an affiliate link and complete a purchase on a third-party merchant's website, that merchant—not Allofwind LLC—processes your payment, fulfils your order, and provides the applicable product or service.

The third-party merchant's delivery, subscription renewal, cancellation, refund, return, warranty, privacy, and customer-support policies apply to those purchases. Questions about an affiliate purchase should be directed to the merchant identified on your order confirmation, invoice, receipt, or billing statement.

2. Products sold directly by us

Allofwind LLC may sell selected software products, software licences, subscriptions, digital downloads, access credentials, and related digital services directly through this website.

The applicable product page will state the product description, price and currency, licence or subscription period, system requirements, delivery method, estimated delivery time, and any product-specific limitations known to us.

Product names and trademarks belong to their respective owners. Unless expressly stated and supported by written authorisation, references to a third-party brand do not mean that Allofwind LLC is the brand owner or that the brand sponsors or endorses this website.

3. Digital delivery

Direct-sale products are delivered electronically. Depending on the product, delivery may include:

  • A download link sent to the email address used at checkout.
  • A software licence key or activation information.
  • Access credentials or an invitation to an online account.
  • Instructions for redeeming or activating a subscription.
  • Access to files or services through a customer account.

Unless a product page states otherwise, delivery normally begins after payment has been successfully authorised and any required fraud or compliance checks have been completed.

Some orders may require manual review. Delays may occur where payment verification, identity confirmation, regional eligibility, product availability, or additional customer information is required.

Customers are responsible for providing a valid email address and checking spam or junk folders. If an order has not arrived within the delivery period shown on the product page, contact us before submitting a payment dispute.

4. Order accuracy and compatibility

Before completing a purchase, customers should review the product edition, licence term, supported operating system, language, geographic or regional restrictions, account requirements, and other technical information shown on the product page.

We are not responsible for compatibility issues that were clearly disclosed before checkout. Contact us before purchasing if you are uncertain whether a product is suitable for your device, account, organisation, or intended use.

5. Refund eligibility

Refund requests for products sold directly by us are assessed individually. Subject to applicable law, a refund may be approved where:

  • The customer was charged more than once for the same order.
  • We are unable to deliver the purchased product within the stated delivery period.
  • The delivered product is materially different from the description shown at checkout.
  • A licence key or access credential supplied by us is invalid and we are unable to provide a working replacement.
  • A technical issue caused by us prevents access to the purchased product and cannot be resolved within a reasonable period.
  • A refund is otherwise required under applicable consumer protection law.

6. Products that have been delivered or activated

Because digital products can be copied, redeemed, downloaded, activated, or otherwise consumed immediately, change-of-mind refunds may not be available after:

  • A software licence key has been revealed, delivered, or redeemed.
  • A subscription or account has been activated.
  • A digital file has been downloaded or accessed.
  • A personalised or customer-specific product has been created.
  • A service has already been substantially performed.

This limitation does not exclude any refund, replacement, or other remedy that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable consumer protection law.

7. Refund requests

To request a refund for a purchase processed directly by Allofwind LLC, email contact@webdailylifetyles.com and include:

  • The email address used for the order.
  • The order number or transaction reference.
  • The name of the product purchased.
  • A clear explanation of the issue.
  • Relevant screenshots or error messages, where applicable.

Refund requests should normally be submitted within 14 calendar days of purchase or delivery. Requests submitted later may still be reviewed where the product is defective, was not delivered as described, or applicable law provides a longer period.

We normally acknowledge refund requests within two business days. Additional time may be required to investigate activation, delivery, payment, or account records.

8. Approved refunds

Approved refunds are normally returned to the original payment method. We generally cannot redirect a refund to a different person, card, bank account, or payment method.

After a refund is issued, the payment provider or financial institution may require additional processing time before the funds appear in the customer's account.

Where a refund is granted, the associated licence, subscription, download permission, account access, or service entitlement may be cancelled or deactivated.

9. Subscription cancellations

Where we sell a recurring subscription directly, the product page and checkout page will disclose the billing frequency, price, renewal terms, and available cancellation method.

Unless otherwise stated, cancelling a subscription prevents future renewal charges but does not automatically refund charges that have already been processed. Access may continue until the end of the current paid billing period.

Subscriptions purchased from a third-party merchant must be cancelled through that merchant.

10. Unauthorised or disputed payments

If you believe a payment made directly to us was unauthorised, contact us promptly at contact@webdailylifetyles.com . We may request information reasonably necessary to identify and investigate the transaction.

Please contact us before initiating a chargeback when the issue relates to delivery, activation, compatibility, cancellation, or a refund request. This gives us an opportunity to locate the order and resolve the issue.

Nothing in this policy limits a customer's right to contact their bank or payment provider regarding a genuinely unauthorised transaction.

11. Abuse and fraud

We may reject a refund request where there is reasonable evidence of fraud, refund abuse, unauthorised resale, account sharing, licence misuse, false information, or an attempt to retain access to a product after receiving a refund.

We may suspend delivery while an order is undergoing fraud, sanctions, payment, identity, or compliance review.
