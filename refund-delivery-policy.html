Refund, Cancellation & Digital Delivery Policy

Terms for third-party affiliate purchases and products sold directly by Allofwind LLC.

Effective date: 6 August 2026

1. Affiliate purchases

When you purchase after following an affiliate link, the destination merchant—not Allofwind LLC—processes payment and fulfils the order. The merchant's delivery, renewal, cancellation, refund, warranty, and support policies apply.

2. Products sold directly by us

Allofwind LLC may sell selected software products, licences, subscriptions, digital downloads, access credentials, and related digital services directly. Each product page should state the description, price and currency, term, requirements, delivery method, and important limitations.

3. Digital delivery

Delivery may include a download link, licence key, activation information, account invitation, redemption instructions, or customer-account access. Delivery normally begins after successful payment authorisation and any required risk or compliance review.

4. Compatibility and order accuracy

Customers should review edition, licence term, operating system, language, regional restrictions, account requirements, and technical requirements before purchase.

5. Refund eligibility

Subject to applicable law, a refund may be approved for duplicate charges, non-delivery, a materially misdescribed product, an invalid licence or credential that cannot be replaced, or a technical issue caused by us that cannot be resolved within a reasonable time.

6. Delivered or activated products

Because digital products may be copied, redeemed, downloaded, or activated immediately, change-of-mind refunds may not be available after a key has been revealed, a subscription activated, a file downloaded, personalised fulfilment completed, or a service substantially performed. This does not exclude rights that cannot legally be excluded.

7. Refund requests

Email contact@webdailylifetyles.com with the order email, transaction reference, product name, explanation, and relevant screenshots. Requests should normally be submitted within 14 calendar days of purchase or delivery. We normally acknowledge requests within two business days.

8. Approved refunds

Approved refunds are generally returned to the original payment method. Processing time depends on the payment provider or financial institution. Associated licences, access, or subscriptions may be deactivated.

9. Subscription cancellations

Direct recurring subscriptions will disclose billing frequency, renewal terms, and cancellation method before checkout. Cancellation normally stops future renewal charges but does not automatically refund charges already processed.

10. Unauthorised payments

Contact us promptly if you believe a direct payment was unauthorised. Nothing limits a customer's right to contact their bank or payment provider.

11. Abuse and fraud

We may reject requests where there is reasonable evidence of fraud, refund abuse, unauthorised resale, account sharing, licence misuse, false information, or an attempt to retain access after refund.
