Support center

Start with the seller shown on your order.

The correct support path depends on whether you purchased from a third-party merchant or directly from Allofwind LLC.

Email Allofwind DigitalRefund & delivery policy

Third-party merchant order

Checkout occurred after an affiliate link.

Contact the merchant named on the receipt or billing statement for:

  • Order status and fulfilment
  • Licence or subscription delivery
  • Refunds, cancellations, and renewals
  • Tax invoices and payment questions
  • Product warranty or technical support

Direct order

Allofwind LLC appears as the seller.

Email us with:

  • Order email and transaction reference
  • Product name and purchase date
  • Delivery or activation issue
  • Relevant error message or screenshot
  • Requested resolution

Protect sensitive information

Do not include secrets in a support email.

Never send

Full card numbers, security codes, account passwords, government ID unless specifically requested through a secure process, or reusable activation credentials.

Safe to include

Order reference, masked payment descriptor, product name, purchase date, non-sensitive screenshots, and a description of the problem.

Response target

Within two business days, Monday–Friday, 9:00–17:00 New Zealand time, excluding public holidays.

Common direct-order issues

Delivery, activation, compatibility, and refund requests.

Check spam folders, confirm that the order email is correct, review the product requirements, and retain the receipt. Contact us before initiating a chargeback when the issue can be investigated through normal support.
